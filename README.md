## Märunen Ideas:
* They are six.
* They are the "*gods*" of the story.
* They aren't evil, but good.
* They created the world.
* They also created the dust woman*¹, but they didn't want the dust woman to get evil.
* As they notice, that the dust woman was getting evil, they created Giladonve, Grafnah and To and called them "the three great children".

## Giladonve Ideas

### Giladonve's parents:
* Giladonve is one of the children of the six Märunen.
* Giladonve is the child of the Märune of nature and weather.
* Because Giladonve is really a "*god*".
* Giladonve got a human body of the Märunen.
* Giladonve is in her god shape mightier than in her human shape.
* She is the mightiest child of the Märunen.

## Tolverion Ideas

### Tolverion's parents:
* Tol is one of the children of the six Märunen.
* Tol is the child of the Märune of peace and war.
* Because Tol is really a "*god*".
* Tol got a human body of the Märunen.
* Tol is in her god shape mightier than in her human shape.
* He is the least mighty child of the Märunen.

# Tolverion's childhood (in his human shape):
* Tol spent his childhood with the Breschner*³.
* Tol learned there what the Breschner know how to do.
* Tol learned to wield a light sword in his human shape at the age of 6.
* Tol got his first light amour at just under 12 years old.

### Tolverion's human shape:
* He has got a copper coloured amour.
* He has got a morning star.
* He has got a copper coloured shield.

### Tolverion's "god" shape:

#### Look:
* His hair is blood red.

#### In a battle:
* He can create temporary illusions of oneself.
* He can create a magic shield. 


## Grafnah Ideas

### Grafah's parents:
* Grafnah is one of the children of the six Märunen.
* Grafnah is the child of the Märune of life and death.
* Because Grafnah is really good.
* Grafnah got no human body of the Märunen.

### How Grafnah became evil:
* The dust woman apparently loved him, so he fell in love with her.
* Then the dust woman started telling Grafnah lies about the Märunen, the bad world, that had to be destroyed, etc.
* Because of that Grafnah want to destroy the world.
