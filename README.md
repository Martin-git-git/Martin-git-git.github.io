## Märunen Ideas:
* They are six.
* They are the "*gods*" of the story.
* They aren't evil.
* They created the world.
* They also created the DW*¹, but they didn't want the DW to get evil.
* As they notice, that the DW was getting evil, they created G, Gr and To and called them "the three great children".
